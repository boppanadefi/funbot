## Technical Context & Invariant Constraints

1. **Simulation Mode & Bankroll Isolation (Section 7.1):**
   - Active simulated sub-wallet capped at $25.00 notional (~0.161 SOL at $155 SOL).
   - Cold segregated reserve ($75.00) locked with zero programmatic access.
   - Mandatory emergency exit reserve: $\ge 0.005$ SOL retained post-trade.
   - Deterministic RFC 8032 Ed25519 simulated keypair producing valid 64-byte signatures for Mainnet wire transactions without requiring real funds.

2. **Dual-Path Dry-Run Simulation:**
   - **Path 1 (Solana RPC Simulation):** Transmits compiled wire transaction to Helius `simulateTransaction` with `sigVerify: false` or simulated signature to inspect compute units consumed, simulated program logs, and account modifications without broadcasting.
   - **Path 2 (Jito Bundle Validation):** Validates bundle packet encoding, atomic transaction ordering, Invariant 6 tip instruction positioning (strictly post-swap), canonical tip recipient account validation, and the 1.5% notional tip cap.

3. **Latency & Regional Profiling:**
   - Active latency probing across Helius RPC/WSS and all 5 Jito Block Engine regions (Frankfurt, Amsterdam, New York, Tokyo, Mainnet default).
   - Dynamic selection of the lowest-latency regional endpoint for production routing.
