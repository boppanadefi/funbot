## Technical Context & Invariant Constraints

1. **Micro-Capital Live Execution ($3.00 Entry Notional):**
   - Active micro-trading operates strictly under **Section 7.1 Bankroll Isolation**:
     - Total bankroll: $100.00 ($25.00 active sub-wallet, $75.00 cold reserve, 0.005 SOL emergency exit floor).
     - Individual position size: **$3.00 notional** (~0.01935 SOL at $155 SOL/USD).
     - Controlled live testing executes only when the **12-Check Pre-Flight Decision Gate** evaluates to `PASS`.

2. **Invariant 2: Path A+ (100% Dynamic Trailing Single Exit):**
   - Position sizing < $50 prohibits multi-tranche exits to eliminate fee dilution.
   - 100% of tokens are liquidated in a single atomic exit swap once the dynamic trailing stop or profit hurdle (+8% to +10% net floor) triggers.
   - Strictly 2 swaps total per trade cycle (1 buy + 1 sell).

3. **MEV & Transaction Structuring:**
   - Jito bundle tips are strictly capped at **1.5% of trade notional** (~0.00029 SOL for a $3.00 trade).
   - Tip instruction is always appended after the swap instruction within the Jito bundle.
   - Dual-Path execution: Uses real Jito bundles when an armed keypair is present; falls back to Helius `simulateTransaction` + bundle payload validation in simulation/dry-run mode.

4. **Friction Attribution & Slippage Decomposition:**
   - Real-world fills are compared against theoretical constant-product AMM formulas:
     $$\Delta y = \frac{y \cdot \Delta x \cdot (1 - f)}{x + \Delta x \cdot (1 - f)}$$
   - Decomposes trade performance into:
     - **Gross Alpha:** Price movement without fees.
     - **Venue Fee Drag:** 0.25% PumpSwap protocol fee.
     - **Execution Slippage Drag:** Difference between pre-flight quote and confirmed on-chain execution.
     - **MEV Friction:** Priority fee + Jito validator tip.
     - **Sandwich Detection:** Flags adverse reserve manipulations within the execution block.

5. **28-Day Autonomous Walk-Forward Validation Gate:**
   - Evaluates whether real-world execution adheres to the 5 production criteria:
     1. Net Expectancy > 0.0 SOL after all friction.
     2. TP1 / Trailing Stop Win Rate $\ge 60.0\%$.
     3. Maximum Drawdown $< \$10.00$.
     4. Realized vs Model Slippage Variance $< 2.0\%$.
     5. Cumulative Return on $25 Active Bankroll $> 0.0\%$.

### Wave 2: Real-World Slippage & MEV Friction Attribution Engine (`src/live/friction_attribution.py`)
- [x] **Task 2.1: SlippageAttributionEngine**
  - Implement `SlippageAttributionEngine`:
    - Compares actual confirmed execution price against theoretical CPAMM price quote.
    - Computes one-way entry slippage:
      $$\text{Slippage}_{\text{entry}} = \frac{P_{\text{fill}} - P_{\text{quoted}}}{P_{\text{quoted}}} \times 100\%$$
    - Computes one-way exit slippage:
      $$\text{Slippage}_{\text{exit}} = \frac{P_{\text{quoted}} - P_{\text{fill}}}{P_{\text{quoted}}} \times 100\%$$
- [x] **Task 2.2: MEV Sandwich Detection & Fee Drag Breakdown**

  - Generates `FrictionAttributionReport` decomposing:

    - `venue_fee_pct`
    - `slippage_drag_pct`
