**Strict Invariants Enforced:**
- **Guardrail 2 (Path A+ 100% Dynamic Trailing Single Exit):** Retains 100% of tokens to capture uncapped tail upside. Stop ratchets to lock in +8% to +10% net once hurdle (+16% to +22% Regime A) is crossed, then trails peak price by 10%–15%. Liquidation occurs in a single transaction (strictly 2 swaps total, 0 tranche dilution).
- **Guardrail 7 (Pre-Trade Risk Capacity & Hard Checks):** Enforce $10 daily loss ceiling, verify position fits within daily capacity before entry, and enforce 20-minute fallback holding timeout.
