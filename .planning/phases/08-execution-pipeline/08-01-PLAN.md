## Technical Context & Invariant Constraints

1. **Invariant 6 (Tip Caps & Bundle Transaction Structuring):**
   - Strict Instruction Ordering: The Jito tip instruction MUST be placed **strictly AFTER the PumpSwap swap instruction**.
   - Tip Cap: Capped at $1.5\%$ of position notional (rejecting or clamping any tip request exceeding 1.5%).
   - Exit Budget Guarantee: Rejects orders if wallet free balance after trade does not maintain at least $0.005\text{ SOL}$ reserved exclusively for emergency liquidations.

2. **Solana Wire Format Compliance:**
   - Versioned / Legacy transaction wire serialization with compact-u16 header encoding.
   - Compute budget instructions: `SetComputeUnitLimit` (e.g. 250,000 units) and `SetComputeUnitPrice` (micro-lamports).
   - Idempotent Associated Token Account creation: `createAssociatedTokenAccountIdempotent` included when user ATA does not exist.

3. **Dual Confirmation Pipeline:**
   - Primary: WebSocket `signatureSubscribe` with commitment level (`confirmed` / `finalized`).
   - Secondary fallback: HTTP RPC `getSignatureStatuses` polling every 1.5 seconds.
   - 30-Second confirmation watchdog timing out stuck transactions.
