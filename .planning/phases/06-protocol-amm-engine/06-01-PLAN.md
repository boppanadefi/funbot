### Wave 2: Constant-Product AMM Math & Sell Quote Simulation (`src/amm/quote_calculator.py`)
- [x] **Task 2.1: Constant-Product Swap Math ($k = x \cdot y$)**
  - Implement exact bonding curve formulas:
    - Buy: $\Delta y = \frac{y \cdot \Delta x_{eff}}{x + \Delta x_{eff}}$ where $\Delta x_{eff} = \Delta x \cdot (1 - \text{fee})$.
    - Sell: $\Delta x = \frac{x \cdot \Delta y}{y + \Delta y}$, net SOL = $\Delta x \cdot (1 - \text{fee})$.

  - Implement `get_buy_quote(virtual_sol, virtual_token, sol_in, slippage_bps=200) -> BuyQuote`:

  - Implement `get_sell_quote(virtual_sol, virtual_token, tokens_in, slippage_bps=200) -> SellQuote`:
    - Computes expected gross SOL, net SOL after fee, price impact, and `min_sol_output` parameter.
