## The 7 Invariant System Mitigations — Milestone 2 Verification

1. **Sub-Minute Sniping Abandoned:** Age window restricted to 8.0m–25.0m post-graduation (`src/engine/entry_orchestrator.py` Check 1).
2. **Path A+ (100% Dynamic Trailing Single Exit):** Forbids multi-tranche exits for positions $< \$50$. Upon crossing the +15% hurdle, ratchets trailing stop to lock in +8% net profit floor. Liquidates full position in a single transaction (strictly 2 swaps total: 1 buy + 1 sell).
3. **Protocol Buyback Cliff Avoidance:** 8-minute cooldown bypasses structural liquidity collapse where protocol buybacks evaporate.
4. **Pre-Flight Double Gate:** Dynamic liquidity floor checks enforce $\le 1.5\%$ price impact on a $\$3.00$ test swap before committing live capital.
5. **Cluster & Fresh Burner Wallet Exclusion:** VUW $\ge 1.20$, Aggression $> 2.0$, Net demand $\ge \$100/\text{min}$, revoked freeze/mint authorities, and top-10 non-curve retail holders $\le 25.0\%$.
6. **Tip Caps & Transaction Structuring:** Jito tip instruction placed **strictly after** the swap instruction; $1.5\%$ notional tip cap; $\ge 0.005$ SOL exit reserve floor.
7. **Pre-Trade Risk Capacity & Daily Circuit Breaker:** -$10.00 daily loss halt, single active position concurrency, and 20-minute holding timeout.
